# FAQ

## Is this official?

No. The Modding Tavern is a fan project. It isn't made by or affiliated with Alta. Alta's servers shut down, and this keeps the game playable.

## Can you give me the game?

No. We can't share the game's files. You need your own copy, from when the game was still around. If you've lost it, you can ask the community.

## Does it cost anything?

No. The launcher, TavernLib and the mods on TavernMods are free and open source. If you rent a VPS to host on, that's between you and your provider.

## Which headsets work?

On PC, anything that works with SteamVR, and Meta Quest or Rift headsets through the Meta Quest Link app (with a cable or Air Link). A standalone Quest version is on its way. See [What you need](../play/getting-started/what-you-need.md).

## Can I play without a headset?

**Fly** is a flatscreen mode for flying around a world, but it's only for server staff. A server has to give you the `fly` role first.

## Why don't I need a password?

Instead of a password, each server gives you a **token**, saved on your PC. It proves who you are on that server. That way, a server owner never sees a password you might use somewhere else.

The catch: lose your token and you lose your account on that server, until its admin resets it. See [Pick your name and headset](../play/getting-started/name-and-headset.md).

## Is my username the same everywhere?

No. Every server keeps its own accounts. Your name on one server says nothing about another. Someone else might even have it.

## Is the launcher a virus? My antivirus says so.

It's made by a small team, isn't signed with an expensive certificate, and changes game files, so some antivirus programs get suspicious. It's open source, so anyone can see exactly what it does: [TavernLauncher on GitHub](https://github.com/ModdingTavern/TavernLauncher). Only ever download it from there.

## There's loads of red text in the logs. Is it broken?

Probably not. The game logs a lot of errors even when it works fine. Errors about web calls to `alta.com` are the old servers that don't exist any more, and are safe to ignore.

## How do I update?

The launcher asks when there's an update. Click **Yes** and it restarts itself. Setup's button flashes if MelonLoader, TavernLib or the patch need updating. See [Staying up to date](../play/staying-up-to-date.md).

## Can I host a server?

Yes. Start with [Before you host](../host/before-you-host.md).

## Can I make mods?

Yes. Start with [Making mods](../mod/welcome.md).
