# Privacy

This site doesn't use cookies, accounts, ads or tracking, and it doesn't keep a record of who visits.

## Searching

- **The search box** looks through the docs in your own browser. It downloads small pieces of the search index from this site, and nothing you type is sent anywhere for that.
- **Related results.** When you type a few words, the search box also asks our search service for pages related to what you typed. That service runs on Cloudflare. It turns your search into numbers that capture its meaning, and compares them with the docs. It doesn't log or store your search, and doesn't know who you are. The only thing kept is the answer to that exact search, for a day, so the same search doesn't have to be worked out again.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/), which handles every request to it, the way any web host does.
