# Play A Township Tale

A Township Tale's official servers are gone. The Modding Tavern keeps the game alive with community servers, and **TavernLauncher** is how you get on them.

By the end of these pages you'll have:

1. The launcher installed.
2. Your game set up to join community servers.
3. A name picked, and your first server joined.

It takes about 10 minutes, plus however long your downloads take.

## Before you start

You need **your own copy of the game**. The game can't be downloaded any more, and we can't share it. If it's still on your PC from when the game was around, you're good. [What you need](getting-started/what-you-need.md) shows you how to check.
