The two logs helpers usually ask for:

- `MelonLoader\Latest.log` in your game folder: which mods loaded, and errors.
- `%APPDATA%\A Township Tale\Client\logs\unity-log.csv`: the game's own log when you play.

To open a folder, press `Win+R`, paste the path and press Enter. If you host, the server's log is in a different place for each way of hosting.
