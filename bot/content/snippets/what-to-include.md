Everyone helping is a volunteer, so tell them up front:

- what you were trying to do, and what happened instead
- the exact error message, copied or as a screenshot
- your launcher version, shown at the top of the launcher
- your headset: SteamVR, Quest over Link, or the standalone Quest
- your logs (try `/snippet logs`)
