Never post these, and nobody helping you needs them:

- anything in `%APPDATA%\TheModdingTavern\tokens`
- your console token or `console_token.txt`
- `server_secret.key`
- `users.json`, which has every player's token in it

Check screenshots for them before you post. Anyone who asks for them is up to no good.
